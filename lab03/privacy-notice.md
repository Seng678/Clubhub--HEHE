# Clubhub Privacy Notice

## 1. Who We Are
Clubhub is a student club and event management application developed by Team HEHE.

## 2. What Data We Collect and Why
- **Student ID, Name, and Email (Required):** Used to register you for clubs and campus events and send essential registration confirmations.
- **Email Reminders (Optional):** Used only if you explicitly opt in to receive upcoming event reminders.
- **Phone Number (Optional):** Used only if you explicitly opt in to SMS reminders.

## 3. Data Minimisation & Log Safety
We do not collect sensitive personal identifiers such as national ID numbers, home addresses, or academic grades. Application logs record only a masked version of your Student ID (e.g., `Student{id=e2******7}`) and never log your name, email, or phone number.

## 4. Your Rights and Consent Choices
- All optional notifications start **off** by default.
- You may withdraw SMS or email reminder consent at any time. Withdrawing SMS consent immediately deletes your stored phone number from the system.
- You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at the end of any semester by contacting the club administrator.
